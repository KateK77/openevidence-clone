What changed

Chinese output and model setup

A hidden local Gemini-key prompt, explicit connection check, bilingual capability notices and a literature-only submit label. Generated Chinese prose is checked, retried once and withheld if it still fails.

Auditable answer revisions

Generate a new answer from the original evidence snapshot using current preferences. The original answer, citations and bookmark remain intact. Access is restricted to the owner.

Claim support and evidence boundaries

Source membership, exact quotations and numeric consistency precede automated claim review. Evidence-gap sections are reviewed as well. A second model pass is not independent clinical validation.

Availability beyond the local PC

The public test site runs on Render Free with a separate Neon Free PostgreSQL database. A single-worker cache profile supports the small instance; Redis remains available in the multi-service deployment recipe.

Next acceptance steps

  2. Save Chinese, ask a new research question and manually inspect the Chinese claims against their original-language source quotations.
  3. Generate a new revision of a literature-only answer; confirm the old answer and bookmark remain intact.
  4. Public HTTPS, reload persistence and account isolation now pass. A timed idle/wake cycle and backup/restore drill remain to be tested.

Practical limits

All testers share the server’s model quota. A draft and review normally require two model calls, with a possible third for a language retry. The application does not activate billing or a paid fallback. The operator must confirm account tier and model availability.

Evidence is limited to available abstracts and metadata. Chinese terminology mapping is bounded. Automated language, numeric and claim checks are imperfect; no independent clinical validation, public load benchmark or security audit was performed.
